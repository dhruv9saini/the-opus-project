# Score provenance

The compositions and printed source editions represented here are public domain. Each LilyPond transcription is released under CC0-1.0. The exact source identifier, URL, and downloaded-file digest live in the work's `metadata.json`.

The catalog is currently empty. A work is not listed as Step 1/5 until its first transcription encodes the complete printed music, including voices, text, dynamics, articulation, and pedal markings, and has been compared measure by measure with the source.

Steps 2–4 must be independent transcriptions made by different agents. Step 5 compares all four against the printed source and publishes the reconciled `score.ly`.
